Returns & Exchanges

30-Day Returns

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery, unworn, unwashed, with original tags attached. We stand behind our product — if something isn't right, let us know.

How to Return

Email hello@blankgolf.com with your order number and reason. We'll send a prepaid return label within 2 business days.

Exchanges

We don't process direct exchanges. Return your item and place a new order for the correct size or style. This keeps things simple and gets your new item to you faster.

Refunds

Refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of receiving your return. You'll receive an email confirmation when your refund is issued.

Sale Items

Final sale items cannot be returned or exchanged. Items marked as final sale will be clearly indicated at checkout.

Damaged or Wrong Items

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, contact us immediately at hello@blankgolf.com. We'll make it right — no questions asked.
